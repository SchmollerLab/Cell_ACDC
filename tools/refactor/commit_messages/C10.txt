refactor: split guiWin into mixins

The methods of guiWin (gui/main_window/window.py) move, byte-exact,
into <Topic>GuiElements mixins in gui/main_window/<topic>.py
(layout, menus_actions, toolbars, keyboard, modes, frame_data,
image_display, drawing_tools, segmentation, tracking,
cca_annotation, lineage_tree, loading, saving, exports, ...).
The window class keeps its bases list (QMainWindow first, then the
window-only mixins, then the mixins shared with the viewer), its
Signals, Qt overrides, __init__ and the methods that call super();
module constants move to gui/main_window/constants.py (_font is
read and written through the module).

Every attribute of guiWin resolves to the same function as before
(MRO snapshot), the methods are unchanged (fingerprints).

Verified with tools/refactor/run.py check: plan_check, the static
fingerprint compare (every item byte-equal or rewritten to the same
new home, other edits listed in out/<commit>/changes.jsonl), the MRO
snapshot of guiWin and MeasurementsViewerWin, the headless entry
points, flake8 E9/F63/F7/F82 and the push tier of the tests.

Old import paths keep working through cellacdc/_compat.py and the
generated cellacdc/_compat_moves.py (same objects, a MovedWarning,
a DeprecationWarning, on use; patches of moved names reach their new
home); Cell-ACDC itself switches to the new paths in the reference
rewrite commit.
