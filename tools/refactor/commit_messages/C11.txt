refactor: split the measurements viewer window into mixins

The methods of MeasurementsViewerWin move, byte-exact, into
Viewer<Topic>GuiElements mixins in
measurements_viewer/main_window/<topic>.py (ui_setup,
configuration, data_io, navigation, image_panel, plotting,
columns, processing, phases, timepoints, custom_annotations), and
the widget-free MeasurementsViewerUtils splits into viewer_utils.py,
position_data.py and annotation_data.py. The viewer specification
(the old module docstring) is the docstring of the
measurements_viewer package.

Every attribute of MeasurementsViewerWin resolves to the same
function as before (MRO snapshot), the methods are unchanged
(fingerprints).

Verified with tools/refactor/run.py check: plan_check, the static
fingerprint compare (every item byte-equal or rewritten to the same
new home, other edits listed in out/<commit>/changes.jsonl), the MRO
snapshot of guiWin and MeasurementsViewerWin, the headless entry
points, flake8 E9/F63/F7/F82 and the push tier of the tests.

Old import paths keep working through cellacdc/_compat.py and the
generated cellacdc/_compat_moves.py (same objects, a MovedWarning,
a DeprecationWarning, on use; patches of moved names reach their new
home); Cell-ACDC itself switches to the new paths in the reference
rewrite commit.
