refactor: split apps.py

The dialogs of apps.py move, byte-exact, to shared/dialogs/
(base, generic, file_selection, metadata, image_tools,
set_measurements, combine_measurements, preprocessing,
model_selection, model_params, tracker_params) when several parts
use them, to gui/mixins/dialogs.py when only the GUI mixins shared
with the viewer use them, and to the dialogs module of their
single program part or utility otherwise (gui/dialogs/,
launcher/dialogs.py, data_prep/dialogs.py,
data_structure/dialogs/, segmentation_module/dialogs.py,
utils/<utility>.py). apps.py is a split shim.

Verified with tools/refactor/run.py check: plan_check, the static
fingerprint compare (every item byte-equal or rewritten to the same
new home, other edits listed in out/<commit>/changes.jsonl), the MRO
snapshot of guiWin and MeasurementsViewerWin, the headless entry
points, flake8 E9/F63/F7/F82 and the push tier of the tests.

Old import paths keep working through cellacdc/_compat.py and the
generated cellacdc/_compat_moves.py (same objects, a MovedWarning,
a DeprecationWarning, on use; patches of moved names reach their new
home); Cell-ACDC itself switches to the new paths in the reference
rewrite commit.
